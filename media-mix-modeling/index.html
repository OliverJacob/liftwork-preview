Planning

Media planning that drives business outcomes.

Comprehensive single-channel and cross-channel media plans, built from your data with advanced predictive modeling.

How it works

The planning process.

Four steps: connect your data, model it, review the recommendations, and put the plan to work.

  1. 01

    Connect

    We take exports from your sales systems and ad accounts. No new tracking, no data team required.

  2. 02

    Model

    The model learns how each channel drives revenue, where its returns diminish, and what mix fits your goal and budget.

  3. 03

    Review

    Fresh reads arrive continuously, written as clear recommendations rather than raw statistics.

  4. 04

    Act

    Budget moves with confidence, and the next round of results sharpens the model.

The plan

The media plan.

Every plan specifies the channels, the budget split, the audience, and the expected ROAS. Scenario modeling shows the projected result of any budget change before it is made.

What you get

Planning capabilities.

Channel mix

One channel or many, chosen for the goal and the budget, not by habit.

Budget allocation

The split across channels, set by the model rather than last year's plan.

Reach and frequency

How many of the right people, how many times, before waste sets in.

Expected ROAS and cost targets

The numbers the plan commits to before launch.

Scenario planning

The expected result of a budget shift, shown before the money moves.

Reliable forecasts

Projected revenue, and LTV where it applies, that becomes the target.
