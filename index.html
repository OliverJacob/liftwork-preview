How we work

Plan. Launch. Optimize. Measure.

Every engagement follows the same discipline, from planning through measurement.

  1. 01

    Plan

    Planning starts with the model. Media mix modeling and geo-level analysis set the channel mix and budget allocation, refreshed continuously rather than once a year.

    MMM•Geo-level Modeling•Always-on Refresh•R&F•Zero-based Budgets

  2. 02

    Launch

    The brief becomes a built campaign. AI agents construct campaigns in platform through direct APIs and MCP integrations, verify conversion tracking end to end, and launch on approval.

    Direct Platform APIs•MCP Integrations•Tracking Verification•Launch QA

  3. 03

    Optimize

    AI monitors every account continuously, catching pacing, tracking, and performance problems early, and reallocates budget and refines targeting within the approved plan.

    Budget Pacing•Anomaly Detection•Bid & Audience Refinement•Creative Rotation

  4. 04

    Measure

    Every engagement reports through one framework: ROAS by channel and campaign, single-tactic and cross-tactic attribution, conversion reporting and CRO, and incrementality testing that feeds the next plan.

    ROAS•Single & Cross-tactic Attribution•CRO•Incrementality
